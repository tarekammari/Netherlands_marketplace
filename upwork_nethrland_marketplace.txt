Looking for a Next.js + PostgresSQL + Stripe Connect developer — student/enterprise task marketplace
We're building a two-sided marketplace connecting university students in the Netherlands with companies looking to outsource short tasks (research, design, data analysis, marketing, etc.). Think of it as a structured, contract-first alternative to generic freelance platforms.

• Clean, minimal design similar to skill-bid.vercel.app
